Does this optional cache stay a lookup, not a second Discord?

Question: what optional cache gives useful identity lookup (same snowflake, last snapshot) without making Client, Decode, Rest, or interactions depend on a live in-memory Discord model? Handlers always receive the decoded payload. Cache is an extra map the Client may fill after Decode. It never auto-fetches, never grows .reply() methods, and is off by default.
